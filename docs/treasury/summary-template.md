### Quarterly Roadmap
Focusing on the three goals we have, (1/ the best paid writers, 2/ most trusted content for readers, 3/best UX for both writers and readers) the Aikin team proposes the next quarters product roadmap. Based on user feedback we propose the following enhancements to the Nuance product over the coming 12 weeks:
- Complete the UX overhaul. Complete replacement of front-end.
- Integrate fiat payments via Stripe
- Email newsletter subscription via Mailerlite
